## Keyboard keys

Press {{W}} to move forward and hold {{Left Shift}} to run. Open the map with {{M}} and close it with {{Esc}}.

## Combinations

Combinations are joined with a plus, and each key gets its own cap: {{Ctrl+Shift+K}}, {{Alt+Enter}}. For a plus key itself, write {{+}}.

## Arrow keys

Turn with {{Left}} and {{Right}}, or look with {{Up}} and {{Down}}: the four arrow keys are shown as arrows.

## Mouse buttons

{{Left click}} selects, {{Right click}} draws the bow, {{Middle click}} and {{Scroll}} change the view, and {{Mouse 4}} and {{Mouse 5}} switch it. {{Shift+Right click}} combines a key and a button.

## Gamepad buttons

On a controller, hold {{pad:RT}}, press {{pad:A}} to confirm, and use the {{pad:D-pad Up}} to move up. Give both names for one button when you want to: {{pad:RB}}, {{pad:R1}}.

## Symbols

Keys with a symbol are written as the character and named in plain words: {{;}} (semicolon), {{,}} (comma), {{[}} (left bracket).

## In a table

| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Move forward | {{W}} |
| Run | {{Left Shift}} |
| Rest | {{R}} |

> [!NOTE]
> Keycaps work in tables and captions, but not inside the text of a link.

## Both controllers

Give the Xbox name first and the PlayStation name second, and say so once on the page: hold {{pad:RB}}, {{pad:R1}} to block, and press {{pad:A}}, {{pad:Cross}} to confirm.

## Number pad

{{Numpad 5}} stops, and {{Numpad Enter}} confirms.
