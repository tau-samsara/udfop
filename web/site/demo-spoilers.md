## A spoiler

The final boss is >!the Lich King, who is weak to fire!<, so bring fire spells.

The words are masked by a dark bar. Click them, or press {{Enter}} or {{Space}} while they are selected, to show them, and again to hide them.

## Inside a sentence with formatting

>!**Bold**, *italic* and a [[Controls|link]] work inside a spoiler, and the link does nothing until the words are revealed!<

## Several on a line

The answer is >!seven!<, found in >!the third dungeon!<.

> [!NOTE]
> A spoiler hides words from the eye, not from the page. The text is still in the file on GitHub, and a reader can select it and copy it. Use it for surprises in the game, never for anything private. A spoiler must sit on one line.
