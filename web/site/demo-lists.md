## Bulleted list

- A hyphen, an asterisk or a plus starts a bullet.
- Items can hold **bold**, *italic*, `code` and [[Controls|links]].
- A long item wraps under its own text, so the bullet stays on the left of the first line when the item runs over more than one line of the page.

## Numbered list

1. A number and a full stop or bracket starts a numbered item.
2. The numbers are renumbered from the first one, so a list written 1, 1, 1 still reads 1, 2, 3.
3. Use a numbered list when the order matters, such as steps.

## Nested lists

- Skills
  - Martial
    - Short Blade
    - Long Blade
  - Arcane
- Attributes
  1. Strength
  2. Intelligence

## A list after a paragraph

Text, then the list straight after it:
