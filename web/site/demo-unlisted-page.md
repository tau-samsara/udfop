This page has `unlisted: true` in its front matter. It is not in the Game Guide lists or in its category, and it has no breadcrumb above the title. It still opens from a link, such as the one on [[site:Demo: Front matter]], and the editor can open it.

Use it for a page that other pages link to but that does not belong in the lists, such as the glossary's data page.
